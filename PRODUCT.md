# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Developers who use coding agents and need an independent check of AI-generated changes before merge or deployment. They arrive to understand what Proof Agent verifies, which usage path fits their workflow, and what its verdicts mean.

## Product Purpose

Proof Agent documents an adversarial verification system that separates the code-producing worker from the verifier. The site succeeds when a developer can understand the trust model, choose the repository, GitHub Action, command-line, or Grok Bot path, and interpret the resulting evidence without inflated claims.

## Brand Personality

Technical, skeptical, direct. The voice should feel like a careful reviewer: concise about the mechanism, explicit about limits, and unwilling to treat confidence as evidence.

## Anti-references

Generic SaaS landing pages, excessive card grids, decorative grid backgrounds, inflated AI claims, and unrelated visual spectacle. The site must not hide operational details behind marketing language.

## Design Principles

- Put evidence and operational details ahead of claims.
- Make the independent worker/verifier boundary impossible to miss.
- Keep working usage paths close to the explanation they support.
- Use compact, varied rhythm so developers can scan without losing reading order.
- State experimental limits with the same prominence as capabilities.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast, preserve visible keyboard focus, respect reduced-motion preferences, maintain 44px touch targets, and prevent horizontal scrolling at mobile widths.
